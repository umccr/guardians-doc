## Summary

As an omics infrastructure provider, I want to provide omics data as:
* released data (data sent to a collaborator)
* via analysis services (data retained and analysed in-place)
to enable research and satisfy the governance requirements of data owners.

## Sub-requirements

These requirements are a work in progress. Those that are complete
will be marked as such.

We have split them into two broad categories - requirements of the
"users" of the service, and technical requirements for services
needed by the "operators" of the service.
