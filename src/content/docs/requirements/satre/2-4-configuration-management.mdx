---
title: "🟰 2.4 Configuration Management"
---


Source: [SATRE Specification v2.0](https://satre-specification.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
| Pillar: Computing Technology and Information Security | Capability: 2.4


Genomics does not fundamentally change configuration management requirements. Infrastructure-as-code and automated validation are already standard practice in cloud-based genomics environments.

<div class="not-content">

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.4.01</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have a documented procedure for configuring infrastructure.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.4.02</strong> (Recommended) — You should use configuration management tools to automate configuration.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.4.03</strong> (Recommended) — You should be able to verify whether the configuration is valid.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.4.04</strong> (Recommended) — You should regularly verify your TRE configuration.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.4.05</strong> (Mandatory) — You must be able to replace a non-compliant TRE with a compliant system.</p>
</div>

</div>
