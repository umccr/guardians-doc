title: "🧬 1.3 Risk Management"
---


Source: [SATRE Specification v2.0](https://satre-specification.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
| Pillar: Information Governance | Capability: 1.3


<div class="not-content">

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.3.01</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have a way to quantify risk to understand the underlying severity.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.3.02</strong> (Mandatory) — You must carry out a data processing assessment for all projects requiring a TRE.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-genomic-note">
  <div class="satre-title"><span>🧬</span> Genomic note</div>
  <p>Genomic data processing assessments must give particular weight to risks that, while not exclusive to genomics, are especially pronounced here: (1) immutability — re-identification risk never diminishes over time, (2) familial implications — a participant's data reveals information about biological relatives who may not have consented, (3) high-dimensionality — even "de-identified" genomic data is notoriously re-identifiable through linkage attacks as reference databases grow.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.3.03</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have a process for designing, implementing and recording risk mitigations.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.3.04</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have clear roles and responsibilities relating to risk ownership, escalation and delegation.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.3.05</strong> (Mandatory) — You must understand the risk appetite of your TRE organisation.</p>
</div>

</div>
