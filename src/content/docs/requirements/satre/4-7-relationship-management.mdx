  <p><strong>SATRE (Recommended):</strong> You should have a clear process for stakeholders to feedback on your TRE infrastructure.</p>
  <p class="satre-context mb-2">GitHub issues, Slack, email, or forms.</p>
  <p><strong>Genomics context:</strong> A stakeholder specifically proposed: "An asynchronous text forum and a synchronous text chat channel so that users can coordinate efforts and share learnings and trip over serendipities. This is big enough not to just be one of a Supporting Capability." The genomics research community expects active user communities around shared infrastructure — not just feedback channels. This is a cultural expectation from the bioinformatics community where collaboration and knowledge sharing are the norm.</p>
