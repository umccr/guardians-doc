---
title: "🟰 1.2 Quality Management"
---


Source: [SATRE Specification v2.0](https://satre-specification.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
| Pillar: Information Governance | Capability: 1.2


<div class="not-content">

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.01</strong> (Mandatory) — You must ensure that changes to policies and SOPs can only be made by trusted individuals.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.02</strong> (Mandatory) — You must use versioning and a codified change procedure for all policies and SOPs.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.03</strong> (Recommended) — You should measure the performance of information governance within the TRE.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.04</strong> (Mandatory) — You must audit your TRE organisation against relevant requirements and standards.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.05</strong> (Mandatory) — You must report on and share outcomes of each audit with the required bodies.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.06</strong> (Mandatory) — You must ensure suppliers and contractors align with your security requirements.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.07</strong> (Mandatory) — You must monitor compliance of your suppliers with the terms of the contracts.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.08</strong> (Mandatory*) — You must track and maintain any physical assets used by your TRE.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.09</strong> (Mandatory) — You must log, track and resolve any issues from deviations, incidents and audit findings.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.10</strong> (Mandatory) — You must use reported issues to inform changes for process improvement and risk management.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.11</strong> (Recommended) — You should collect and maintain quality management data for measuring TRE effectiveness.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.2.12</strong> (Optional) — You could use a QMS to standardise and automate quality management tasks.</p>
</div>

</div>
