---
title: "🟰 1.6 Training Management and Delivery"
---


Source: [SATRE Specification v2.0](https://satre-specification.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
| Pillar: Information Governance | Capability: 1.6

<div class="not-content">

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.01</strong> (Mandatory) — You must determine what training is relevant for all roles within the TRE.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.02</strong> (Mandatory) — You must ensure relevant training is available for all roles.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.03</strong> (Mandatory) — You must provide repeat or updated training where necessary.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.04</strong> (Mandatory) — You must maintain accurate training records tied to role and access levels.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.05</strong> (Recommended) — You should accept proof of relevant training certifications from trusted third parties.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.06</strong> (Optional) — You could have a training platform delivering online training in various formats.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.07</strong> (Optional) — You could implement a learning management system (LMS).</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.08</strong> (Optional) — You could ensure courses are available in standard, transferable formats.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.6.09</strong> (Optional) — You could keep historical copies of courses to demonstrate competency at a point in time.</p>
</div>

</div>
