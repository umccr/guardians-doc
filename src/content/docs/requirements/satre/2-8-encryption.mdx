---
title: "🟰 2.8 Encryption"
---


Source: [SATRE Specification v2.0](https://satre-specification.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
| Pillar: Computing Technology and Information Security | Capability: 2.8


<div class="not-content">

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.8.01</strong> (Mandatory) — Your TRE must encrypt project and user data at rest.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.8.02</strong> (Mandatory) — Your TRE must encrypt data in transit between the TRE and external networks.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.8.03</strong> (Recommended) — Your TRE should encrypt data in transit inside the TRE.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.8.04</strong> (Recommended) — You should use encryption algorithms and software that are widely accepted as secure.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>2.8.05</strong> (Recommended) — Your TRE should use secure key management.</p>
</div>

</div>
