Source: [SATRE Specification v2.0](https://satre-specification.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
| Pillar: Information Governance | Capability: 1.5


<div class="not-content">

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.5.01</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have a robust method for identifying accredited members prior to data access.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.5.02</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have clear onboarding processes for all roles within your TRE organisation.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.5.03</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have services to manage access to resources based on identity.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.5.04</strong> (Mandatory) — You must not give anyone access to datasets without agreement from the Data Controller.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.5.05</strong> (Mandatory) — You must have robust and secure applications to authenticate users and services.</p>
</div>

<div class="satre-unchanged">
  <p><span>🟰</span> <strong>1.5.06</strong> (Mandatory) — You must give each user a unique logon with changes to records strictly controlled.</p>
</div>
