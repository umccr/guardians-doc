Our project was in part an innovation project - in the spirit of this - we explored
a variety of technologies that could build our overall vision. Each individual
technology exploration or project was part of a larger work package - most of which
have been situated on the architecture diagram below. Some work packages however
were orthognal to the larger architecture or were pure technology plays - these are
listed in a section at the bottom of this page.

#### Main Work Packages

    <Card title="COST-MONITOR" icon="dollar">
        Tools and exploration of ways to accurately monitor or report on costs

        cost estimate for copying 1 TB of data to a new location - giving the data
        custodian or researcher a sense of how much various activities might cost
        _before_ they happen.
