Our project was in part an innovation project – in the spirit of this - we explored
a variety of technologies that could build our overall vision. Each
technology exploration or project was part of a larger work package – most of which
have been situated on the architecture diagram below. Some work packages however
were orthogonal to the larger architecture or were pure technology plays – these are
listed in a section at the bottom of this page.
