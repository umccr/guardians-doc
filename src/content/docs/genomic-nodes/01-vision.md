---
title: Vision
---

The genomic node concept envisages a trusted third party infrastructure operator
that can be trusted by data holders to provide a secure and reliable location
for researchers to perform genomic analysis.


Our genomic node concept is based on a Goldilocks Principle - to find
and propose an operating model for genomics infrastructure that is
"just right".

one single designated infrastructure provider starts with a non-competitive
environment encouraging rent seeking and slowing innovation

HOWEVER

having every research group and institute that deals with genomic data
spin up their own genomic processing infrastructure - and inviting researchers
to use the infrastructure will lead to silos, and potentially inward
facing environment suitable only for the researchers of that institute.

Our proposal is a middle ground - more than one
provider of infrastructure, but less than many. A genomic node
infrastructure provider would be looking to provide competitive offerings - be
that in terms of security and accreditation, types and scale of
compute available or pricing models. Genomic nodes could be centred around
particular cloud offerings or institute compute.

The genomic node provider operates in conjunction with a data holder and
nominated data custodians to provide a control plane for managing
access to the data *within their node*. Data holders in turn establish
a trust relationship with the genomic node provider (be that contractual
or otherwise) and allow their datasets to be staged onto the
node infrastructure for use by researchers.

Researchers and data holders retain exactly the same data agreements
as they would in the current state - the only difference is that
the venue for the research is now offered in a third party environment.
