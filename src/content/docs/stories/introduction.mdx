## Our Context

We are the Platform Team at CCGCM (the [Collaborative Centre for Genomic Cancer Medicine](https://genomic-cancer-medicine.unimelb.edu.au)) -
a centre formed of genomic cancer researchers from the
[Peter MacCallum Cancer Centre](https://www.petermac.org)
and the [University of Melbourne](https://www.unimelb.edu.au).

Our centre does a mix of clinical and research genomic sequencing - with a variety
of assays. We also do novel cancer research, both within the core
team of the centre and in associated research groups.

## Our Stories

Our work on GUARDIANS data sharing is motivated by solving
underlying stories we encounter each day. Not all our stories
are national focussed - some can be solved by a simple mouse click in
existing software. We thought we would start this work
with an enumeration of our existing sharing
stories just as a flavour for our data sharing space - these
will not all be dealt with in the rest of this documentation.

### Story "Sequencer level"

"I am a collaborator with the centre that has our own
account in the ICA (the Illumina Connected Analytics) system
attached to our Illumina sequencer -
albeit maybe under a different organisation/tenant. I
would like some sequencing data or pipeline runs from
ICA placed directly into my account."

### Story "Downloadable links for HPC"

"I am a researcher at an external university. I do not have any cloud experience
and would prefer to do my research on a HPC. Can you provide me with
links or a script so that I can download the data I need?"

### Story "Adhoc send it to my cloud"

"I am a collaborator within an external institution that
sends data or samples to CCGCM for clinical curation reporting.
We would like a copy of the analysis data that
is generated from the curation reporting as part
of our research governance/requirements on an ad-hoc basis. Can you
send it to a cloud account our IT department has set up?"

### Story "Routinely send it to my bucket"

"I am a postdoctoral researcher working at CCGCM in a research group. We sequence
 samples regularly, and I track sequencing runs I order and request the corresponding
 FASTQ files when I expect the results to be available. Our group manages a dozen
 ongoing projects; when data is requested, please push it to a defined, configured
 location in AWS."

### Story "Internal use of data"

"I am a bioinformatician working on some methods development
 at CCGCM. I am a University of Melbourne employee and have familiarity
 with AWS. CCGCM has sequenced a patient
 that has exhibited some edge cases I want to test with my software.
 This is patient data so I need to do work in a privileged
 AWS account and not somewhere other developers do work."

### Story "Data subsetting"

"I am a new PhD researcher working for a research group
 at CCGCM and am employed by Peter Mac. I have lots of bioinformatics
 experience but very little cloud compute. It has been decided
 I will be given access to a small subset of project data for
 my initial project work and that I will be writing the workflows
 in nextflow in Seqera. I am only going to be allowed to access
 the variant data from the participants."
