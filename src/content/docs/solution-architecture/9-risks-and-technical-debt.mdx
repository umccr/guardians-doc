## Risk register

Likelihood and impact are rated Low / Medium / High.

| ID    | Risk                                                                                         | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation                                                                  |
|-------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|--------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `R-1` | Sharing relies heavily on AWS-specific features (VPC Lattice, bucket VPC conditions), limiting non-AWS researcher environments | Medium | Medium | Keep htsget as the interface so other transports can be added; investigate [Globus](../../technology/globus) and pre-signed download scripts for HPC |
| `R-2` | Egress and thaw costs grow faster than expected                                              | Medium     | High   | Cost estimates before copies; [costing model](../../reports/costing-data-sharing-platform/costing-scrollytell); budgets |
| `R-3` | Entitlements in REMS and grants in AWS (Lattice associations, bucket policies) drift apart     | Medium     | High   | The REMS entitlement model is not as soph        |
| `R-4` | Small team means key-person dependency for bespoke components                                 | High       | Medium | Open-source components, documentation, infrastructure as code                 |
